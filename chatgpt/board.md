# Board adapter — sequential, not fake parallel agents

Original: `skills/founder-board/SKILL.md`, `skills/founder-board/lenses.md`.

Input: business description, buyer, product, sales channel, initial price (or MISSING), region and constraints. For low-data products accept MISSING price/budget and do not invent them.

1. Write a neutral idea brief without a verdict.
2. Apply the original **Offers** framework alone; list strongest point, top 3 failure modes, questions, conditions, subjective score 1–10, FUND/FUND IF/PASS.
3. Set that output aside. Apply original **Monopoly** framework independently to the same brief, then original **Product** framework independently. These are sequential *perspectives from one model*, **not** three independent AI agents.
4. Compare disagreements, recurrent risks, and conditional actions. Include at least one viable competing explanation for the idea's apparent value.
5. Distinguish subjective scores from empirical measurements. The numeric average is a qualitative aid, not a probability or forecast.

Output: `idea brief`, three lens memos, consolidated conditions and recommendation for competitor research. Do not claim the published authors said, voted or endorsed anything. For missing market facts, point to `competitors.md`; for missing finances, `cfo.md`.
