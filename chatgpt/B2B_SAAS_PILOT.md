# Low-data B2B SaaS pilot — neutral template

**Purpose:** determine whether further customer discovery is worth the effort, not certify a forecast. This is an example intake and is safe to store publicly because it contains no actual business records.

## Owner input (do not fill blanks with guesses)
- One-sentence product / specific painful task:
- Target first buyer (role, team size, country):
- Current alternative / manual workaround:
- What is already demonstrated in product:
- Actual number of paying customers and actual revenue (or MISSING):
- Price hypothesis, monthly costs and acquisition channel (each OWNER_REPORTED or MISSING):

## Stage outputs
1. Board: three separately applied lenses + risk conditions.
2. Competitors: sources for direct, indirect and spreadsheet/manual substitute products.
3. Pricing: candidate packages and public competitor benchmarks; real payment interest still MISSING until tested.
4. CFO: start with conditional break-even based on sourced/owner-reported costs. If growth, churn or price assumptions are missing, **do not** publish 12-month profit as a business fact.

## Decision gate
- Do at least three verifiable alternatives (including manual workflow) exist?
- Can we state one material improvement that a prospect can actually observe?
- Do we know the approximate customer role and what they currently spend in money/time?
- Does a conservative break-even scenario need an implausible customer count?
- Can we ask 5–10 independent target prospects for a demo/paid pilot with a clear yes/no signal?

Verdicts: EXPLORATORY (idea mapped), TESTABLE_HYPOTHESIS (specific buyer/test), PARTIALLY_VALIDATED (real observed signal), INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Do not use a simulated panel result as customer validation.

## Privacy boundary
No customer lists, non-public revenue, confidential screenshots or proprietary application files belong in this public fork. Put real case evidence in a private workspace and reference only aggregated/non-sensitive observations in any public write-up.
