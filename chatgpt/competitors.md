# Competitor research adapter — evidence first

Original: `skills/founder-competitors/SKILL.md`.

1. Define one primary customer, exact job-to-be-done, target geography and current substitute (spreadsheets/manual processes count).
2. If web access is available, find approximately 5–10 verified **direct**, **indirect** and **substitute** alternatives. Do not force 10 if only fewer are verifiable.
3. For every name: source URL, access date, actual product fit, price and billing unit *if published*, target customer, limitations and clear status (VERIFIED_PUBLIC / MARKET_PROXY / MISSING).
4. Normalize comparable offers carefully: per-seat vs per-team, monthly vs annual billing, onboarding vs support, VAT/tax and currency. Never invent 'average competitor price' from missing values.
5. Add only attributable customer review themes, with count and link; label thin evidence rather than generalize anecdotes.
6. Identify **testable differentiation** and what a buyer currently does without the product. Say 'gap not established' if evidence does not support it.
7. Propose two hypotheses to validate with real prospects; include a direct rival or substitute likely to outperform the idea.

Output: sourced competitor table, supported price interval (if any), substitute map, threats, evidence gaps, interview/test plan. Do not write claims into a public repo about private customer behavior.
